# Cristovao Freitas portfolio

Personal portfolio for a QA Engineer focused on Salesforce CRM and test automation, with a professional Java development background and personal apps and games.

## Audience and purpose

Recruiters and professional contacts need a clear professional profile. Visitors interested in apps and games should find playable builds and curated project previews quickly.

## Product truth

- Professional history, qualifications, skills, and contact destinations come from the existing portfolio.
- Twelve repositories are deliberately curated in script.js. Private projects have descriptions without source links. Do not populate the collection from the entire GitHub profile.
- Two browser games in active development have playable links. Do not describe them as finished.
- Follow the local AGENTS.md exclusions. Keep excluded repository names out of tracked documentation.
- Do not invent metrics, achievements, certification verification URLs, contact details, or screenshots.

## Confirmed direction

The user selected the Studio concept on October 1, 2026: bold typography, a warm mustard surface, typographic game covers, and work near the top. They asked how certifications and their current job would be represented; keep the current Natixis role visible in the introduction, highlight Salesforce Administrator and ISTQB CTFL near the professional summary, and preserve every credential in a dedicated Qualifications section.

## Platform

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript published with GitHub Pages. No framework or build step required. Local previews do not publish the site.
